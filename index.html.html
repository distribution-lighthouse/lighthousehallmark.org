Our Story

An East African Vision for Quality Alcohol Distribution

Lighthouse Hallmark Ltd was born from a simple but powerful conviction: that every retailer, hotel, and hospitality outlet across East Africa deserves access to quality alcohol brands — delivered reliably, priced competitively, and backed by a partner they can trust.

We set out from Moi's Bridge, Kenya, with two warehouse locations and a growing network of supplier partnerships. What began as a focused distribution operation has steadily expanded, with new offices in Nyahururu, extending our reach deeper into the Rift Valley corridor and Central Kenya highlands.

Today, Lighthouse Hallmark is an authorised distributor for some of East Africa's most trusted alcohol brands — from homegrown Kenyan producers to internationally recognised names. We carry over 22 products across vodka, brandy, whiskey, beer, and lifestyle beverages, and we are actively onboarding new supplier partners to bring even greater choice to our clients.

Our commitment is not just to distribute — it is to illuminate the path for every business we serve. Like a lighthouse, we stand as a dependable beacon: visible, steady, and always guiding our partners toward growth.

Founded — Moi's Bridge, Kenya

Established with a vision to bring organised, accountable alcohol distribution to the Rift Valley and beyond.

Partnership with Two Cousins Distillers

Became an authorised distributor for J Movers Vodka and J Movers Brandy.

Partnership with Swam Wines Ltd

Opened a second warehouse in Nyahururu, extending reach into Central Kenya.
